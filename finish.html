Q&A

What does the price include?

The price includes inlaying service and side diamonds, but not the cost of the main stone. Please go to ‘search diamonds’ page to view the diamond details (or learn about the purchasing process). You are welcome to visit our store for more styles and try on the ring samples.

How to measure ring size?

Please watch our education video and download the ring size chart for reference. In addition, you may contact us to borrow ring sizer for accurate measurement.

Is there any additional charge for mounting or plating service?

Unlike other jewelry shops, we have no hidden charges. Price listed includes the costs of side stones, mounting and plating.

How long does it take for the ring to be set?

It takes only 2 working days if the ring setting is in stock; while it takes 2-4 weeks if it is made to order.

Is custom-made service available?

Yes. You may customize your favorite style by simply providing relevant pictures or hand drawings. For details, please refer to exclusive custom-made service.
